## 1. Observed evidence (facts, as of 2026-08-16)

- Live `/pioneers` dashboard: **8 joined · 5 completed · 5 Founding claimed / 100**.
- Of the **5 completed**, **2 are the owner's own verified accounts** and **3 are unverified**.
- Additionally, **3 verified users joined but did NOT complete** the Quest.
- **Therefore:** there is currently **no independently-verified external completer** sufficient
  to validate organic product demand. The headline "5 completed" does **not** mean 5 genuine,
  independent, verified pioneers.
- The `tec.pi` domain auction has ended but the **Claim is still Pending** — Pi ties the final
  claim to **organic usage**, which makes any inflation of participation metrics actively
  counterproductive (a review liability, not an asset).
- **The current bottleneck is acquisition/activation — but the underlying CAUSE is not yet
  established.** The page itself can record completion (proven), so the problem is not the page.

## 2. Decisions (approved, active)

1. **Interview-first:** contact the **3 verified Pioneers** who joined but did not complete.
2. Ask **one** friction question with selectable reasons (a–e) — one-character reply.
3. **Zero product coding** until those responses are collected and interpreted.
4. The primary entry CTA is **"Enter TEC"**, **not** "Claim" (and not "Reserve" if reserving
   would imply holding one of the 100 before it is earned).
5. **Founding Pioneer status remains earned only after Quest qualification** — consistent with
   the TEC constitution (earned, not given: Zone "verified", Legend outcomes, Elite criteria).
6. **No Founding number (`#X`) is allocated merely by entering** — `#X` is assigned only on
   qualify. Framing while exploring: *"You're in the running for Founding Pioneer #X — complete
   the Quest to claim it."*
7. **Do NOT build/activate** (paused): Founding Pioneer Wall · Referral loop · Airdrop / financial
   incentive · any artificial amplification of participation metrics.
8. **Primary validation KPI becomes: Independent + KYC + Completed.**
9. `joined` remains a **diagnostic** metric, **not** the primary success metric.
10. The current **"First 100"** positioning stays technically intact, but is **not** to be treated
    as proof of demand, and is **not** the primary growth message right now.
11. **Reconsider growth loops (Wall / Referral) only AFTER** evidence of genuine independent demand
    (working target: ~15–20 real, verified pioneers before any viral mechanic ships).
