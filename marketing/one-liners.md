first line of any post. Honesty rules in `README.md` apply. App lines mirror the

## Platform — TEC

**One line (EN):** TEC is a full economy on Pi — 24 apps, one identity, one wallet,
real Pi payments.

**One line (AR):** TEC اقتصاد كامل على Pi — 24 تطبيق، هوية واحدة، محفظة واحدة، مدفوعات Pi حقيقية.

**Two lines (EN):** TEC turns Pi into a real economy: commerce, assets, business,
reputation, and more — 24 apps sharing one identity and one wallet. Log in once with
Pi and the whole ecosystem is yours.

**Founding 100 (EN):** Be one of the first 100 Founding Pioneers. Open every TEC app in
Pi Browser, and earn a permanent Founding badge in your reputation. Earned, never bought.

**Founding 100 (AR):** كن من أول 100 Founding Pioneer. افتح كل تطبيقات TEC في متصفح Pi،
واكسب شارة Founding دائمة في سمعتك. بتتكسب، متتشريش.
