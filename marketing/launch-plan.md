## Principle — earn attention in the right order

Lead with the apps whose value is instantly obvious and fully live, so the first
impression is "this is real." Introduce preview apps honestly, as previews. Every post
ends with the **Pioneers** CTA so the whole campaign feeds one funnel (C-133).

## The waves

**Wave 0 — Foundation (before any app post)**
- Publish the **Founding 100** post (the anchor campaign). Pin it.
- Publish the **Invite & Earn** post. These two run continuously underneath every wave.
- Have `faq.md` + `whats-live.md` ready to link when anyone asks "is this real?".

**Wave 1 — The proof (live, instantly understandable)**
- **Hub → Commerce → Ecommerce → Analytics.** One post each, spaced out (not all at once).
- These are the credibility anchors: registered Pi App IDs, real payments. Lead here.

**Wave 2 — The ecosystem (live)**
- Zone · Explorer · NBF · Connection · Life · Alert · Nexus · Assets · System · DX ·
  NX · Titan · Epic · Legend · Elite · VIP · Estate.
- One per slot; group by theme when it helps a narrative (e.g. reputation trio
  Legend → Elite → VIP on consecutive days).

**Wave 3 — The gated previews (honest framing)**
- FundX · Insure · Brookfield — post the **preview** variants only. Lead with what you
  *can* do (browse / learn / see your risk) and state plainly what's gated.

## Cadence (suggested)

| Slot | Post |
|------|------|
| Always pinned | Founding 100 + Invite & Earn |
| Day 1 | Hub |
| Day 2 | Commerce |
| Day 3 | Ecommerce |
| Day 4 | Analytics |
| Then ~1 app/day | Wave 2 apps (theme-grouped) |
| Interleave weekly | Re-share Founding 100 + Invite & Earn + one FAQ answer |
| Last | Wave 3 previews (honest) |

- **1 app per slot.** Flooding 24 posts at once buries them and reads as spam.
- **Reply, don't just broadcast.** Answer "is this real?" with a link to `whats-live.md`.
- **Bilingual.** Post EN + AR to the right channels (see `launch-posts.md`).
- **Never** front-run a status: if `whats-live.md` says gated/preview, the post says so.

## Per-channel notes

- **X/Twitter:** 3–4 short lines, one link. Thread only for the Founding 100 explainer.
- **Telegram / Pi chats:** the post + the extra Pioneers CTA line; drop `whats-live.md`
  when trust comes up.
- **Pi community forums:** lead with the FAQ "how is this different" answer, then the app.
