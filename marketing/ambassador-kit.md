# TEC Ambassador Kit

For anyone representing TEC in the Pi community — Telegram, X, chats, meetups. This is
the operational form of the community layer in `C-133 §9–§10`. Read `README.md` first;
the honesty rules there are binding on ambassadors too.

## What TEC is (say it in one breath)

> TEC is a full economy on Pi — 24 apps, one identity, one wallet, real Pi payments.
> You log in once with Pi and the whole ecosystem is yours.

## The pitch, three lengths

- **10 seconds:** "TEC turns Pi into a real economy — 24 apps, one login."
- **30 seconds:** "TEC is 24 apps on Pi sharing one identity and one wallet — commerce,
  assets, business, reputation, and more. The first 100 people to try every app earn a
  permanent Founding Pioneer badge. It's early, it's real, and it's on Pi Mainnet."
- **2 minutes:** Walk through the funnel — log in with Pi → open apps in Pi Browser →
  each app does something real (buy, verify, build) → complete the quest → Founding
  badge in your reputation. Point them to hub.tecosystem.app/pioneers.

## Talking points (all true, all defensible)

- **One identity, one wallet.** Not 24 logins — Pi SSO carries across every app.
- **Real payments.** Apps use Pi payment contracts; nothing is a mockup where it says live.
- **Earned, not bought.** Founding badge, Elite recognition, Legend reputation — earned.
- **Honest by design.** Counters show real numbers or zero. We never fake traction.
- **Pi does KYC.** We don't verify identity ourselves; Pi handles wallet + KYC.
- **Built to a constitution.** Every app has a charter; money is gated to one service.
- **Invite & Earn is a free month, not a payout.** Invite a friend; when they take
  their first subscription, you both get a free 30-day PRO month. It's a subscription
  reward — we don't pay Pi for referrals.

## Do

- Use the exact one-liners and launch posts in this kit (they're pre-checked).
- Say **"preview"** for any app that isn't fully live (see `pi-portal-copy.md`).
- Link the Pioneers page: `hub.tecosystem.app/pioneers`.
- Answer "is it safe?" with the FAQ: `hub.tecosystem.app/pioneers/faq`.
- Invite people to *try*, not to *invest*.

## Don't

- ❌ Don't invent numbers — users, downloads, "spots left", revenue. Ever.
- ❌ Don't promise returns, yield, profit, or protection (FundX/Insure/Brookfield).
- ❌ Don't say recognition/badges can be bought.
- ❌ Don't claim we KYC or verify people ourselves.
- ❌ Don't promote an app whose experience is an empty page — mark it preview.
- ❌ Don't create false urgency or fake scarcity.
- ❌ Don't pitch the referral as "earn Pi" / "Pi cashback" / "X π per invite" — the
  reward is a **free PRO month**, and it unlocks only on the friend's first
  subscription (ADR-012). A Pi-denominated referral payout is hard-gated; it doesn't exist.

## Handling common questions

| They ask | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Is this a scam / another airdrop?" | "No token airdrop. It's working apps on Pi — try one and see. Numbers we show are real." |
| "Do I have to pay?" | "No. The Founding badge is earned by using the apps, not paid for. Some apps have optional Pi purchases, always with your approval." |
| "Is my data / wallet safe?" | "You log in with Pi itself — we never see your password or keys. Sessions are secure cookies. See the FAQ." |
| "Why so many apps?" | "It's an economy, not one app — commerce, assets, reputation, business. Each does one real thing." |
| "What do I get for being early?" | "A permanent Founding Pioneer badge in your reputation, and early access to new apps. Earned by completing the quest." |

## If you're unsure

If a claim isn't in this kit and you can't back it with a real number or a live feature,
**don't say it.** When in doubt, under-promise. Our credibility with the Pi Core Team is
the single most valuable asset we have (C-133 §7).
