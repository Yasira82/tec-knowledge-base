# Outreach & DM Templates

Short, honest first-touch messages for the three audiences that grow TEC. Direct
messages, not broadcasts — personalise the first line. Honesty rules in `README.md`
apply: no fake urgency, no promises, no fabricated numbers. When they ask "is this real?"
send `whats-live.md`; for objections, `faq.md`.

> **Rule:** one clear ask per message. Open a conversation, don't close a sale. If they're
> not interested, thank them and move on — reputation compounds, spam destroys it.

---

## 1) Merchants / sellers (→ Commerce · Ecommerce)

**EN**
```
Hi {name} — saw you sell to the Pi community. TEC has a live Pi-native storefront +
checkout (real Pi payments, not a demo): list products, take Pi, manage orders.
Free to start. Want the 2-minute walkthrough link?
```
**AR**
```
أهلًا {name} — شفت إنك بتبيع لمجتمع باي. TEC عنده متجر ودفع Pi حقيقي شغّال (مش تجربة):
اعرض منتجاتك، استقبل باي، إدِّي أوردراتك. مجاني تبدأ. تحب لينك شرح دقيقتين؟
```
Follow-up if they engage: send `commerce.tecosystem.app` + the Commerce launch post.

---

## Do / Don't for outreach

- ✅ Personalise line 1. ✅ One ask. ✅ Link only after they say yes. ✅ Bilingual to match them.
- ❌ No mass identical DMs to strangers. ❌ No "limited spots!" unless it's the real 100 cap.
- ❌ Never promise earnings, yield, or "get rich" — TEC is tools, not an investment.
- ❌ Don't imply official Pi endorsement — TEC is built *on* Pi, not run by Pi.
