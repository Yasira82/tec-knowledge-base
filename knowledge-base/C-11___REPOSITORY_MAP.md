## 3. Read the table, not the assumption

- **The Hub does not depend on `@yasser172/tec-ui` or `@yasser172/tec-auth`.** Its
  `tec-frontend/package.json` has never listed either (git history of that file); it carries
  its own auth and colour tokens. Its `CLAUDE.md` says otherwise — the code is the evidence.
- **Assets · Commerce · Ecommerce pin `@yasser172/tec-ui` 2.1.0** — deliberately, until their
  re-skin (C-02 §2). Every other domain app is on `^3.0.0`.
- **Ranges, not installs.** A `^1.2.2` resolves to whatever the lockfile holds; the version on
  `main` of the package is in "Packages".
