## Capability and TEC AI

TEC AI does not invent institutional execution.
TEC AI consumes governed capabilities.

TEC AI **may:**
- Select Capabilities
- Combine Capabilities
- Recommend Capabilities
- Explain Capabilities

TEC AI **may NOT:**
- Create Certified Capabilities
- Approve Capabilities
- Govern Capability Execution

> Institutional execution remains capability-governed.
