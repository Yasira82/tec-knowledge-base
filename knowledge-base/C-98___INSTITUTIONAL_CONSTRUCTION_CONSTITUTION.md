## DX Constitutional Scope

DX (System of Construction & Expansion) **may:**
- Package capabilities into distributable SDKs
- Generate scaffolding from templates
- Automate verification pipelines
- Distribute reference implementations
- Create developer toolchains

DX **may NOT:**
- Certify capabilities (C-94 governance process)
- Approve new constitutional knowledge (C-95/C-99)
- Override construction verification gates
- Deploy to production without certification

---

## Construction and TEC AI

TEC AI **may:**
- Generate construction artifacts (code, config, scaffolds)
- Recommend construction patterns
- Detect construction anti-patterns
- Generate from reference implementations

TEC AI **may NOT:**
- Deploy constructed systems without governance approval
- Create new certified capabilities outside C-94 lifecycle
- Bypass construction verification gates
